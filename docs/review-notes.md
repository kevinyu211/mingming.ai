## Chat rebuild, 2026-09-04 (owner feedback: "robotic, too big, transcription doesn't work")
- **The briefing stopped being a form.** 明唔明？ with a 明白 button after every card is gone. `buildBeats()` in components/chat/briefing.ts turns the cards into a flat script — greeting, 「先講最緊要嗰樣」, one bubble per red flag, ONE teach-back question in words with no button behind it, a quiet connective per run of card type (「跟住講藥。」, once for three medicines), then a closing line — and app/chat/page.tsx plays it with a typing-dots pause between beats. The reader takes the floor by holding the bar; nothing else stops it. Constitution II is now a property of the beat array rather than of JSX placement, and it is pinned by unit tests.
- **The amber block became amber bubbles.** WarningBlock.tsx is deleted. Each red flag is its own bubble in the same column, still first, still auto-spoken, still carrying its own 睇張紙點寫. That is where most of the vertical space went.
- **WeChat scale.** Both sides are bubbles: agent 16.5px on white with a 30px mascot, reader 16px on jade. Message gap 22px → 10px. AgentLimits moved to the TOP of the thread (FR-022 still satisfied — on screen, not behind a disclosure) because the thread auto-scrolls to the newest message, so a block pinned after it was on screen for the entire conversation.
- **Two real transcription bugs, both fixed and both now covered in the browser suite.**
  1. `onPointerLeave` was bound to the release handler and the bar reflows the instant a hold starts, so a press routinely ended itself a frame later — no error anywhere, the question simply vanished. `setPointerCapture` on pointerdown; `pointerup` is the only thing that ends a hold. Test: "a thumb that drifts off the bar keeps recording".
  2. The transcript was drawn inside the 72px button, clipped, beside a nowrap sub-label. It is reported up through `onInterim` and drawn as the reader's own bubble in the thread. Test: "what the microphone hears appears in the thread before anything is sent" (also asserts nothing is sent until release).
- **A latched driver, found by the e2e run.** `runBeat` claims `driving` and releases it in the callback the typing chain runs at the last clause — and cancelling that chain means the callback never runs. The first interruption of a session latched the flag forever and every later `play()` returned at its first guard: 明仔 answered once and went silent for good. `takeFloor()` clears it. Guarded by "a model outage says so plainly and leaves the sheet readable".
- **Per-message speaker** (讀多次) replaced the 再講一次 button: the repeat belongs to the thing being repeated, not to a prompt that has scrolled away.
- **The greeting names the document, not the sheet's filed title.** `sheetTitle()` derives that from the first printed clinic — "SOPD" on the HK fixture — and 「你張SOPD我已經睇咗喇」 is not something a person says. The header keeps the specific title.
- **Changing the spoken language restarts the sheet in it.** Committed messages are a record of what was said and are not rewritten; the thread is cleared and 明仔 says it again from the top. Somebody reaching for the language chip has just told you they did not understand a word of it.
- playwright.config.ts takes `E2E_CHROME` (an executablePath) as an alternative to `channel: "chrome"`, so the suite runs in a container with no Google Chrome.

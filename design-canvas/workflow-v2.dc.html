What changed, and the 跟進 question
1 · 冇播放掣
The agent types itself out and reads aloud with no press. The speaker in the top right kills the voice; the text still types. Nothing in the thread is a play button any more — the 讀住 waveform is a status, not a control.
2 · 一個大框，WeChat 咁
One 72px bar across the whole width: hold to talk, tap to type. Holding past 220ms starts listening; a quick tap opens the keyboard with a 56px field and a 送 button. The language switch moved up top so the bar carries one job.
3 · 通知，唔係留言卡
The block under 拍張紙 is now a notification — 明仔's name, the time, an amber unread dot, and the question itself. Tapping it opens the current sheet's thread, where the check-in is waiting and reads itself out with 食咗 / 未食 underneath. Answer it and the notification collapses into a quiet line that links to 跟進.
4 · 跟進 同張紙點對齊
This was the real gap, so I made the model explicit: one active sheet at a time. 跟進 is not a global list — it is the active sheet's medicines, its appointment, its warnings, and nothing else. So 跟進 now opens with a strip naming the sheet it is following (瑪麗醫院 · 心內科 · 9月1日) which taps straight back into that thread.

Photographing a new sheet replaces the active one; the previous sheet moves into 以前嘅 as read-only history with its counters frozen — which is why those rows are now marked 只可以睇 rather than being tappable. That keeps the promise 「張紙寫：…」 makes: a counter can only ever quote one sheet.
5 · 拍 or 上載
The single action is now split in two equal halves — jade 拍張紙 and a tinted 上載相片 — because the adult child photographing the sheet in the ward and mailing it over is at least as likely as the patient shooting it herself. Upload opens a multi-select picker (tap several, 用揀好嘅 N 張) and lands in the same 檢查 screen, so retake/add-a-page work identically from either route. Six pages is the stated ceiling and it is visible in all three places it can bite — the picker subtitle, the dimmed thumbnails once you hit it, and the camera's own 夠 6 頁喇 hint with a spent shutter. A medical sheet must never be quietly truncated; if the app can't take a page it has to say so.
The loop you described
拍張紙 → 檢查 → 讀住 → 傾偈 talks itself through warnings, medicines, follow-up, diet → she says 明白 and puts the phone down → comes back to 記錄 and finds the notification → taps in → the check-in reads itself → 食咗 → the counter in 跟進 drops to 今日食晒. The reset button under the phone replays it from the start.
